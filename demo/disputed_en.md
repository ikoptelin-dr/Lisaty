# Disputed wording — English page (EU / US)

For a regulatory specialist familiar with EU food law (Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 and related rules) and US rules
(21 CFR 101.93, GRAS). Each item is a question, not a conclusion. None of the regulatory texts was downloaded in this session,
so the page makes no statements about what these regulations say.

| № | Where | Wording | Why it may be a problem | Question for the specialist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X-1 | Headline | “Probiotic bacterial lysates” | In parts of the EU the word “probiotic” is treated as an implied health claim | Can “probiotic” be used to describe the source bacteria in B2B material and on finished-product labels? |
| X-2 | Distributors, “Species safety status” (L-QPS) | “Every species … is on EFSA’s QPS list” | QPS is a pre-assessment tool for EFSA risk assessment, not a market authorisation, and it applies to the organism, not to a lysate | Is the QPS statement acceptable as written, or does it imply an authorisation that does not exist? |
| X-3 | Whole page | Lysates sold as food ingredients in the EU | Whether bacterial lysates of these species are “novel foods” in the EU was not checked | Novel Food status? Is any EU or US marketing route available without a dossier? |
| X-4 | Evidence, “Literature” | ISAPP postbiotic definition (“…confers a health benefit on the host”) | Quoting a definition that contains “health benefit” next to the product | Keep, rephrase, or remove for EU/US buyers? |
| X-5 | Product names | “BifiLyz”, “LaktoLyz”, “PropioLyz”, “FerLyz Pro” | Latin-script spellings were made up for the demo | Plant approval and a trademark check in the target markets |
| X-6 | Documents block | “Allergen statement (milk — whey carrier)”, “Non-GMO statement” | Listed as documents buyers will ask for; whether they exist is unknown | Confirm which statements the plant can actually issue |
| X-7 | Eyebrow | “Koltsovo, Russia” | EU and US buyers will run export-control and sanctions screening on a Russian supplier; the page does not address this | Should the English material go out at all before a compliance review? |
